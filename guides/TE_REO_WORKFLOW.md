### The Key Concepts

**Whakapapa** - More than genealogy

- "Whaka" = cause/make
- "Papa" = layer/foundation
- Together: "to layer" or "to understand relationships"

Used for:

- Family trees (genealogy)
- How systems relate (systems whakapapa)
- How understanding builds (spiral whakapapa)

**Tapu** - More than sacred

- "Tapu" = forbidden, restricted, protected
- Often mistranslated as "taboo" (close, but not quite)
- About _responsibility_, not just restriction

Example:

- A river during spawning season: TAPU (protection for fish)
- Your friend's grief: TAPU (respect the space)
- Kaitiaki's user memories: TAPU (protected knowledge)

**Kaitiakitanga** - The whole reason this exists

- "Kaitiaki" = guardian
- "Tanga" = the practice/philosophy
- Together: "The practice of guardianship"

Includes:

- Responsibility to land/water/community
- Long-term thinking (7 generations)
- Reciprocal relationships (not extraction)

## Part 4: Translating Environmental Concepts

### The Challenge

Environmental terms in English often lose cultural meaning.

English → Te Reo Māori translation:

| English        | Te Reo       | Why Different                          |
| -------------- | ------------ | -------------------------------------- |
| Water          | Wai          | Just liquid? No—wai = life force       |
| River          | Awa          | Just geography? No—awa = ancestor      |
| Health         | Hauora       | Not just "wellness"—includes spiritual |
| Pollution      | Whakaruruhau | Literally "disturbance/harm"           |
| Restoration    | Te hanga anō | Literally "to make again"              |
| Responsibility | Kawenga      | Burden + duty + sacred obligation      |

### In Kaitiaki

When reviewing environmental data:

```
NOT: "The Waikato has high nitrogen levels"

YES: "Te Waikato's mauri is kaupapa (weakened) from
agricultural whakaruruhau. Tainui kaitiaki are working
toward te hanga anō (restoration)."
```

The second version:
✅ Names the iwi (accountability)
✅ Uses te reo (cultural grounding)
✅ Explains impact (mauri concept)
✅ Frames as solution (restoration verb)

## Macron Reference (Quick Lookup)

When adding te reo content, use these properly:

| Letter | Macron | Example               | Meaning         |
| ------ | ------ | --------------------- | --------------- |
| A      | ā      | Āotearoa              | Long "ah" sound |
| E      | ē      | Kēkē                  | Long "ay" sound |
| I      | ī      | Kiwi → Kīwi (correct) | Long "ee" sound |
| O      | ō      | Mōanarangi            | Long "oh" sound |
| U      | ū      | Pūtaputā              | Long "oo" sound |

**Common errors:**

- "Maori" (wrong) → "Māori" (correct)
- "Kaiti" (wrong) → "Kaitiaki" (ambiguous) → "Kaitiaki" with check (correct)
- "Aotearoa" (less correct) → "Aotearoa" (accepted) → "Āotearoa" (most culturally grounded)
