## What Seed Knowledge Is

Seed knowledge is the initial foundation a Kaitiaki profile may begin with.

It is meant to provide:
- grounding
- context
- shared vocabulary
- mission orientation
- initial semantic structure for recall

It should not be hidden or unquestionable.

## What Seed Knowledge May Include

Depending on the profile, seed knowledge may include:
- foundational reo concepts
- whakapapa or relationship mappings
- environmental or historical context
- governance or mission context
- handling and cultural framing

## Rules For Seed Knowledge

Seed knowledge should be:
- explicit
- attributable
- reviewable
- replaceable
- clearly marked as seed/foundation material

It should not:
- silently define truth for every future deployment
- override local/community context without review
- hide provenance

## Relationship To Memory

Seed knowledge is not the same as personal or relational memory.

Seed knowledge provides:
- starting context
- a base layer of concepts

Whakapapa memory provides:
- evolving personal or relational understanding
- notes, summaries, and linked context over time

## Relationship To Tikanga

Seed knowledge may inform language understanding, but it does not automatically become reviewed public-serving `tikanga` truth unless it has passed the appropriate review and promotion path.
