# Free Tier Eligibility

**Mission**: Pack Dashboard is free for people and communities. We charge only to prevent corporate free-riding.

---

## Who Qualifies for Free Tier?

### ✅ Always Free

1. **Individual Users**

   - Personal projects, learning, hobby use
   - No size limit, no time limit
   - Example: artist building a portfolio, researcher organizing notes

2. **Educational Institutions**

   - Schools, universities, colleges
   - Teaching, research, student projects
   - Example: university library cataloging collections

3. **Non-Profit Organizations**

   - Registered 501(c)(3) (US), Charities Commission (NZ), equivalent
   - Mission: public benefit (not shareholder profit)
   - Example: NGO managing community resources, food bank, homeless shelter

4. **Grassroots & Community Groups**

   - Volunteer-run organizations
   - Community commons, cooperatives, credit unions
   - Public benefit + democratic governance
   - Example: community garden, tool library, repair cafe, local food network

5. **Public Institutions**

   - Government agencies (local, regional, national)
   - Public libraries, museums, archives
   - Example: city council managing civic data, public library

6. **Small Business** (First 2 Years)
   - <10 employees
   - <NZD $500k annual revenue
   - Startup grace period to prove model
   - Example: freelance designer, local bakery, small consulting firm

---

## Who Needs a Commercial License?

### ❌ Commercial License Required

1. **Large Companies**

   - ≥10 employees **OR**
   - ≥NZD $1 million annual revenue **OR**
   - Owned by VC, private equity, or public company

2. **SaaS/Service Offering**

   - Offering pack-dashboard as a service to end users (for fee or ad-supported)
   - Example: SaaS startup selling "smart catalog" feature built on pack-dashboard

3. **Government Contracts**

   - Using pack-dashboard for government contract work (consulting, development)
   - Exception: government agencies themselves (see public institutions above)

4. **Proprietary Deployment**
   - Deploying modified pack-dashboard code without sharing changes
   - (Under AGPL-3.0 copyleft, this requires commercial license OR open-source changes)

- No paid license required

## What If My Status Changes?

**Scenario**: Your startup grows from 5 to 15 employees

1. **You're now ineligible** for free tier (>10 employees)
2. **Email us**: `license@awanet.nz` — we'll discuss options:
   - Option A: Buy commercial license (~1-5% of annual software spend, flexible)
   - Option B: Open-source modified code (free, AGPL-3.0)
   - Option C: Negotiate community rate (case-by-case)

**No penalties** — growth is good! We just want to share in success.

## Enforcement & Transparency

### How We Verify

1. **Spot audits**: Random free tier users (1x per year)
2. **Public records**: Non-profit status, company registry, revenue filings
3. **Self-reported**: You tell us if status changes
4. **Community reports**: Users can flag suspicious use

### What If Someone Lies?

- **First offense**: Written warning + 30-day cure period
- **Second offense**: Free tier revoked; commercial license required
- **Pattern abuse**: Legal action (AGPL-3.0 enforcement)

**Our bias is toward grace**, not punishment. We'd rather help you transition fairly.

### Transparency Report (Annual)

Every January, we publish:

- Total free tier users (by category)
- Revenue from commercial licenses
- Funding allocation (dev, infrastructure, community support)
- See: `TRANSPARENCY.md`

## FAQ

**Q: I'm a non-profit, but we charge users for services. Is that OK?**

A: Yes! Non-profit means democratic governance + reinvest surplus. You can charge users (that's sustainable). Just can't distribute profits to shareholders.

**Q: I'm using pack-dashboard, but haven't declared eligibility. Am I breaking the law?**

A: AGPL-3.0 means if you modify & deploy, you must open-source changes OR buy a commercial license. If you use unmodified (no changes), no license needed. But we ask you to declare your organization type for community census.

**Q: Can my business use free tier if we're <$1M but growing fast?**

A: Yes, for 2 years. Then we recheck. If you hit $1M+ in Year 3, buy a license or open-source changes.

**Q: What if I disagree with the $1M/$10-person threshold?**

A: Tell us! Email: **policy@awanet.nz**. We revisit thresholds annually based on community feedback.

**Q: I'm a freelancer using pack-dashboard to help clients. Is that free?**

A: Depends:

- Using unmodified pack-dashboard: free (you're just a user)
- Modifying it for clients: open-source changes OR commercial license

**Q: Can I pay for premium features instead of a commercial license?**

A: Not currently. But we're exploring tier models (email: **partnerships@awanet.nz**). Feedback welcome.
