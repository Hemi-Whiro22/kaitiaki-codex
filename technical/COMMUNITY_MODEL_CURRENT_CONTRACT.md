# Community Model

Purpose of rewrite:
- Preserve the community, contribution, and sustainability intent without locking it to one dated funding or reward model.
- Keep this as a governance and participation document.

Source document:
- `technical/COMMUNITY_MODEL.md`

Rewrite posture:
- local-first
- anti-drift
- no invention

## Why A Community Model Exists

The system is not only code.
It also needs a clear answer to:
- who can contribute
- how contributions are valued
- how support is sustained
- how community benefit is protected

## Community Principles

The stable principles are:
- contributions should be visible and credited
- community benefit should not be sacrificed for convenience or extraction
- maintainers should be accountable
- sustainability should be discussed openly, not hidden

## Types Of Participation

A good community model can include:
- code contributions
- documentation contributions
- testing and review
- cultural and language review
- research and curation
- funding or sponsorship

These should all be acknowledged as valid forms of participation.

## Sustainability

The original document likely tied sustainability to a specific bounty, sponsorship, or revenue-share structure.
The durable meaning is simpler:
- the project needs a visible, fair way to keep going
- contributors should not be invisibly exploited
- community-support claims should be backed by practice

Different implementation periods may use different financial structures.
The principles should remain stable even when the mechanics change.

## Relationship To Ownership And Transparency

The community model should align with:
- `OWNERSHIP.md`
- `TRANSPARENCY.md`
- contribution and branching guidance

This prevents hidden contradictions between:
- who controls the work
- who benefits from it
- who is allowed to shape it

## Current Translation

The original document’s lasting value is not one exact program or sponsorship model.
It is the idea that the project should:
- say how contribution works
- say how support works
- say how community benefit is preserved

This rewrite keeps that role while avoiding dated operational details pretending to be permanent truth.
