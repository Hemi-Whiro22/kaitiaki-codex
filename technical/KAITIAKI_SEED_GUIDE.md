# 🐺 Kaitiaki Seed Data Guide

## What Is Seed Data?

When a new Kaitiaki instance spins up, it doesn't start blank. Instead, it inherits **collective memory**:

- Te reo Māori learning foundation (full macron support)
- Whakapapa connections between waterways
- Real environmental data (vs. NZ greenwashing)
- AwaNet mission context
- Cultural knowledge tagged as tapu/noa

This is the **whakapapa** of Kaitiaki itself—the genealogy of its purpose.

## The Four Seed Articles

### 1. 🗣️ Te Reo Māori - Foundation Guide

**Status:** Public (tapu_level: 0)  
**Kaupapa:** te_reo_māori, pānui, kupu, whakataukī  
**Whakatauki:** "He reo, he tapu" (Language is sacred)

#### What's In It

Essential te reo Māori vocabulary with **full macron support** (ā, ē, ī, ō, ū):

**Harikoa (Happiness domains):**

- Koa - joy, happiness
- Hari - cheerfulness
- Pūmau - steadfast, enduring

**Whakapapa concepts:**

- Tūpuna - ancestors
- Whānau - family
- Iwi - tribe, people
- Whakapapa - genealogy/layering (the foundation of everything)

**Tapu & Mana domains:**

- Tapu - sacred, restricted, protected
- Noa - unrestricted, free
- Mana - prestige, spiritual authority
- Kaitiakitanga - stewardship/guardianship

**Āhuatanga (Nature terms):**

- Awa - river (living ancestor with mauri)
- Moana - ocean
- Whenua - land
- Ngāhere - forest

#### Why This Matters

NZ tries to marginalize te reo, but it's central to understanding environmental responsibility. By seeding Kaitiaki with te reo _from day one_, we:

✅ Make cultural knowledge accessible  
✅ Normalize macrons in tech  
✅ Build indigenous concepts into the system DNA  
✅ Show that "environment" = "environment within te ao Māori framework"

### 2. 🌊 Whakapapa o Aotearoa Waters

**Status:** Public (tapu_level: 0)  
**Kaupapa:** whakapapa, awa, moana, kaitiakitanga  
**Whakatauki:** "Ko te awa e kaiao nei, he tapu tēnei" (This living water is sacred)

#### What's In It

Genealogical mapping of major Aotearoa waterways:

**Waitemata** ("Face of Waters")

- Auckland's primary water system
- Responsibility: Ngāpuhi, Ngāti Whātua
- Current status: Mauri compromised
- Issues: Urban pollution, stream channelization, silt

**Waikato** ("Biting Waters")

- Waikato Region's lifeblood
- Responsibility: Tainui, Ngāti Raukawa
- Current status: Declining
- Issues: Dairy runoff, hydro dams, temperature changes

**Te Awa Kai** ("Food-bearing Water")

- Southland region
- Responsibility: Ngāi Tahu
- Current status: Threatened
- Issues: Farming impact, fish decline, erosion

#### The Key Insight

**These are not just geographical features. Each awa is a living ancestor with its own mauri.**

Their health reflects OUR stewardship.

When we treat them as commodities (hydro, irrigation, waste disposal), we compromise their mauri.

When we remember them as ancestors (whakapapa), we restore our obligation.

### 3. ⚠️ Clean Green Aotearoa: Reality Check

**Status:** Public (tapu_level: 1 - slightly tapu, critical analysis)  
**Kaupapa:** greenwashing, environmental_impact, data, aotearoa  
**Whakatauki:** "Ko te korero pūāwā: Aotearoa he 'clean and green' - Ko te mōtika: ehara"  
(The lie: Aotearoa is clean and green - The truth: It is not)

#### What The Govt Says

✅ "Clean and green"  
✅ "Least polluted country"  
✅ "Environmental leaders"  
✅ "One of the best environmental performers"

#### What The Data Actually Shows

**Waterway Reality:**

- 57% of monitored rivers unsafe for swimming
- E. coli levels exceed safe limits in 40% of waterways
- Nitrogen & phosphorus rising (agricultural runoff)
- Dairy intensification: 1200% increase in livestock since 1980s

**Biodiversity Crisis:**

- NZ has the **highest extinction rate globally**
- Over 4000 native species at risk
- 50% of native forests lost in last 150 years
- Predators outcompeting native birds

**Climate & Emissions:**

- Agricultural emissions: **47% of NZ total greenhouse gases**
- Methane from livestock: massive contributor
- Forest loss: counteracting reforestation
- Dairy farming: primary driver of waterway degradation

#### Why The Greenwashing Works

💰 Tourism industry depends on "clean & green" image  
💰 Agricultural exports marketed as premium/clean  
💰 International perception lags reality  
💰 Data deliberately aggregated to hide regional hotspots  
💰 Marketing budget >> environmental investment

From te ao Māori perspective:

1. **Honesty** - Acknowledge the mauri is compromised
2. **Action** - Not marketing but restoration
3. **Responsibility** - To iwi and future generations

## Technical Implementation

### Seed Data Storage

```sql
-- In supabase/seed.sql
INSERT INTO artifacts (
  user_id,        -- Demo user ID
  file_name,      -- Identifies the seed content
  kaupapa_tags,   -- Full te reo with macrons!
  tapu_level,     -- 0 (public) or 1 (sensitive)
  whakatauki,     -- Te reo context quote
  summary,        -- English summary
  full_text       -- Full content
) VALUES (...)
```

All text supports **UTF-8 macrons** ✅

### Seed Viewer Component

`src/components/KaitiakiSeedViewer.tsx` displays:

- **Article List** - Browse seed knowledge
- **Detail View** - Read full content
- **Filtering** - By kaupapa/topic
- **Tapu Indicators** - Show sensitive content warning
- **Whakataukī** - Te reo context for each article

### Embedding Integration

Each seed artifact has embeddings pre-computed for:

- Semantic search (user queries match seed knowledge)
- Context enrichment (seed concepts appear in chat)
- Learning recommendations (suggest related content)

## Running Seed Data Locally

When you spin up the dev container:

```bash
npm run dev
```

1. PostgreSQL initializes
2. `seed.sql` runs automatically
3. Seed artifacts and embeddings load
4. KaitiakiSeedViewer component available
5. Full knowledge base ready to explore

View it at: `http://localhost:5173` (if you add it to App.tsx)
